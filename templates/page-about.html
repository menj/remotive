How it started

A company built around one complaint

We kept hearing the same thing from marketing leads across the region. They had three agencies, four dashboards and no one who could say which activity produced revenue.

Re:Motive Media was registered in Singapore in 2024 to answer that. The idea was simple enough to state in a sentence: put senior specialists on the account, keep search, paid, social, content and analytics under one roof, and report on the number the client is actually judged by.

Everyone here worked on brands in this region before joining. That is the whole hiring policy. It means a client briefing does not begin with an explanation of why a campaign that worked in London stalls in Klang Valley.

Why Asia

One region, and none of it interchangeable

Southeast Asia is treated as a single line on a lot of media plans. It behaves like nothing of the sort.

Buyers in Singapore research in English and convert on the site. Buyers in Malaysia often start on Facebook, ask three questions on WhatsApp and never touch a form. Marketplaces carry the purchase in some categories and are irrelevant in others. A plan that ignores those differences spends the same money for a fraction of the result.

We work in the region because that knowledge is not something you acquire from a deck. It comes from running the campaigns, watching where the conversations actually happen, and being close enough to the market to notice when the pattern shifts.

How we work

Built to run light

The way we're structured carries a smaller footprint by default, not as a separate initiative bolted on afterwards.

The team works from wherever the work is, not from a central office everyone commutes into. Specialists plug into your team remotely or sit alongside your agency partners, which cuts out an office's worth of daily commuting and physical overhead before anything else does.

The deliverables follow the same logic. Reports, creative and campaigns are digital from the first draft, so there is no print run, no physical media production and nothing made to be thrown away. It is a lighter way of operating because the model calls for it, not because it is the subject of a separate pitch.
