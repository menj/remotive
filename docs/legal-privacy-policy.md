Re:Motive Media Asia Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202404376G) operates this website. This

is governed by the contract for that engagement.

## Which law applies to you

We are registered in Singapore and we work across Singapore and Malaysia, so
two laws are relevant and which one protects you depends on where you are.

- **Singapore.** The Personal Data Protection Act 2012 applies to how we
  collect and handle personal data as a Singapore-registered organisation.
  The regulator is the Personal Data Protection Commission.
- **Malaysia.** Where we collect or process personal data in connection with
  commercial activity in Malaysia, the Personal Data Protection Act 2010
  applies to that processing. The regulator is the Department of Personal
  Data Protection, Jabatan Perlindungan Data Peribadi.

Where both apply, we follow whichever gives you the stronger protection. If
you are outside both countries, we still handle your data as described here,
and you may have additional rights under your own local law.

## What we collect through this website

**When you use a contact form.** The forms on this site ask for your name,
your email address and your message. Only the email address is required.
Sending the form gives us that information along with the date and time of
submission, the page the form was on, and technical details your browser
sends automatically (your IP address and browser identifier). See "What
happens to a form submission" below for exactly how this is stored and for
how long.

**When you browse.** Our web host records standard server logs for every
visit, which include IP addresses, browser type and the pages requested.
These are kept for [CONFIRM: retention period set by your host, commonly 7 to
30 days] and are used for security and diagnosing faults.

**When you set a colour mode.** Choosing light or dark mode stores a single
value in your browser called `remotive-theme`. It holds nothing but that
preference, it is not sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes it.

We do not ask for, and this website has no way to collect, payment details,
identity documents or any special category of data such as health or
political information.

## What happens to a form submission

Your submission is stored in this website's own database, not only emailed.
We store your name, email address, message, which form and page it came
from, the date and time, the IP address and browser identifier ("user
agent") your browser sent with the submission, whether our notification
email to ourselves sent successfully, and whether our spam filter flagged
the submission. Spam-flagged submissions are still stored, kept separate
from genuine enquiries, and never actioned or replied to.

If [CONFIRM: whether Akismet is connected on this site] Akismet spam
filtering is active, the message content, submitting IP address and
browser identifier are sent to Akismet (operated by Automattic) to check
for spam, under Akismet's own privacy policy. If a form on this site is a
Contact Form 7 form rather than one built into the theme, the same
information is captured into this same store when the plugin reports the
message as sent.

We also send you and ourselves a notification email when you submit, after
your submission is safely stored, so a delivery problem with that email
never causes your enquiry to be lost.

Stored enquiries are kept for [CONFIRM: retention period in months, for
example 24 months] from the date of submission, after which a scheduled
process on the site deletes them automatically. This applies equally to
genuine and spam-flagged submissions.

We use what you send us to reply to you and to prepare for a conversation
about working together. We do not add you to a mailing list on the strength
of a contact form, and we do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

## Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act you may ask us to:

- tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we have used it;
- correct anything that is wrong or out of date;
- delete what we hold, where we have no legal reason to keep it;
- stop using your data for a purpose you previously agreed to.

Write to the Data Protection Officer at the address above. We will respond
within [CONFIRM: response time you commit to; 30 days is the common
standard].

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the regulator
that covers you: the Personal Data Protection Commission in Singapore, or the
Department of Personal Data Protection in Malaysia. Complaining to a
regulator costs you nothing and does not require you to come to us first.

## Keeping data secure

The site is served over an encrypted connection. Access to the inbox that
receives form submissions is limited to the people who need it, and
protected by [CONFIRM: for example, two-factor authentication on the email
accounts].

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data and is likely to
cause you significant harm, we will notify you and the relevant regulator
within the time each law requires: the Personal Data Protection Commission in
Singapore, and the Department of Personal Data Protection in Malaysia where
Malaysian law applies to the affected data.

## Children

This website is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children. We do
not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has
sent us their details, write to us and we will delete them.
